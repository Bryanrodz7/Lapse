# Privacy Policy for Lapse

**Last updated: 16 August 2026**

Lapse is an Android app that tracks expiration and renewal dates — things like a driver's
license, passport, vehicle registration, insurance, professional certificates and warranties —
and reminds you before they lapse.

This policy explains exactly what data leaves your device and why. It is deliberately specific.

---

## The short version

Everything you put into Lapse stays on your phone. The app has no account, no sign-in, no server
and no sync. Nothing you track is ever uploaded to us or anyone else.

Lapse shows a single advertising banner, served by Google. Those ads are personalised, which
means Google receives your device's advertising ID and information about your interaction with
the ad. That is the only data that leaves your device, and you control it — see
[Your choices](#your-choices).

---

## What Lapse stores on your device

All of this is written to app-private storage on your phone and never transmitted anywhere:

- The name, category, expiry date and optional note for each item you track
- The reminder lead times you choose for each item
- Photos you attach to an item, copied into the app's private storage and downscaled
- Your settings: default reminder lead times and theme preference

Lapse has no ability to read this data remotely. There is no account to log into, and no copy of
it exists outside your device.

**Backups.** Automatic cloud backup of Lapse's data to Google Drive is switched off. On Android
12 and later nothing is backed up to the cloud at all; device-to-device transfer is allowed, so
your items move with you when you set up a new phone. On Android 11 and earlier, where the two
cannot be configured separately, photos are excluded from backup.

**Uninstalling** the app deletes all of it, including any photos you attached.

---

## What leaves your device

Lapse itself transmits nothing. The only outbound data comes from the Google Mobile Ads SDK,
which serves the banner at the bottom of the main screen.

| Data | Purpose | When |
|---|---|---|
| Advertising ID (AAID) | Selecting personalised ads, frequency capping, measurement | Every ad request |
| Device and configuration data — model, operating system, language, screen size, network type | Selecting and rendering an ad that fits your device | Every ad request |
| Approximate location, derived from your IP address | Regional ad targeting | Every ad request |
| Ad interactions — impressions and clicks | Billing and measurement | When an ad is shown or tapped |
| Your advertising consent choice | Recording your decision, and passing it to ad partners | Stored on device; sent with ad requests |

Nothing in the first section of this policy is included. Google does not receive the names,
dates, notes or photos of anything you track.

## Advertising partner

Ads in Lapse are provided by **Google** (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC, depending on your
region) through the Google Mobile Ads SDK, which may share data with its advertising partners.

Google's handling of this data is governed by their own policy:

- **Google Privacy Policy:** <https://policies.google.com/privacy>
- **How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services:**
  <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>

Ads are personalised by default where you have consented. Where consent is required — in the
European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland — Lapse asks before any ad is
requested, using Google's User Messaging Platform. If you decline, ads still appear, but they
are non-personalised.

---

## Your choices

- **Change or withdraw advertising consent.** Open **Settings** in Lapse and choose
  **Ad privacy choices**. This reopens the consent form. It is shown where the consent framework
  applies to you.
- **Reset or delete your advertising ID.** Android **Settings → Privacy → Ads** lets you reset
  the identifier or delete it entirely, which stops personalised advertising across all apps.
- **Turn off notifications.** Lapse asks for notification permission the first time you save an
  item. Declining does not disable anything else; reminders simply stay silent. You can change it
  later in Android's app settings.
- **Delete your data.** Uninstalling Lapse removes everything it stored, permanently.

---

## What Lapse does not do

- No account, sign-in or user profile
- No analytics or crash-reporting SDK
- No third-party trackers beyond the advertising described above
- No selling of personal information
- No reading of your contacts, calendar, files or photo library — attaching a photo uses
  Android's system photo picker, which gives Lapse access only to the single image you choose
- No location permission is requested; the approximate location above is inferred by Google from
  your IP address, as it is for any internet request

from them.
