# Lapse

Lapse tracks expiration and renewal dates — driver's license, passport, vehicle registration,
insurance, professional certifications, warranties, memberships — and reminds you before they
lapse.

Everything you track stays on your device. There is no account, no sign-in and no cloud sync.

- **[Privacy Policy](privacy-policy/)**

Lapse is an Android app. Source: [github.com/Bryanrodz7/Lapse](https://github.com/Bryanrodz7/Lapse)
