## Full description

Limit 4000 characters.

Play renders this as plain text, so it uses capitalised section headers and no markdown.

```
Lapse tracks the things that quietly run out — your driver's license, passport, vehicle registration, insurance, professional certifications, warranties, memberships — and tells you before they lapse.

EVERYTHING STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE

No account. No sign-in. No cloud sync. The dates you track, the notes you write and the photos you attach are stored on your phone and are never uploaded. There is no server holding your documents and no profile being built from them, because neither exists.

Attaching a photo of a card or certificate uses Android's system photo picker, so Lapse only ever sees the single image you choose. It is copied into the app's private storage — never to your gallery, never to an external folder.

ADD SOMETHING IN UNDER FIFTEEN SECONDS

The form opens with the keyboard already up. Tap a quick pick — Driver's License, Passport, Vehicle Registration, Car Insurance, Vehicle Inspection — and the name, category and reminder schedule fill themselves in. Dates take one tap too: one year, two years, five years, or choose an exact day.

REMINDERS THAT SAY SOMETHING USEFUL

Decide how much warning you want: 30 days, 7 days, the day before, or your own combination. Notifications name the item and the real time left — "Your passport expires in 30 days", not "Reminder: item expiring". Tapping one opens straight to that item.

RENEW IN ONE TAP

When something is close to expiring, Lapse offers to move it forward by its own renewal term — ten years for a passport, one for a registration — and shows you the exact new date before you accept it.

BUILT TO BE READ AT A GLANCE

The list is sorted by what is closest, grouped into this month, the next three months, and later. The number of days left is the largest thing on the screen, because that is what you actually want to know. Expired items sink quietly to the bottom rather than shouting at you.

A home screen widget shows the single next thing to expire, so you can check without opening anything.

Full dark mode. Readable at large system text sizes. Complete TalkBack labels throughout.

ABOUT THE ADS

Lapse is free and shows one banner at the bottom of the main list. There are no full-screen ads, no video, and nothing that interrupts what you are doing.

Those ads are served by Google and are personalised, which means Google receives your device's advertising ID. Where consent is required, Lapse asks before a single ad is requested, and you can change or withdraw that choice at any time from Settings. Nothing you track is ever shared with advertisers.
```
